Intracranial aneurysms are a common type of cerebrovascular abnormality with high risk upon rupture, which can cause subarachnoid hemorrhage. Although CTA can clearly display parent vessels and aneurysm morphology, manual interpretation remains prone to missed detections or inconsistent judgments due to large slice counts, complex vessel bifurcations, and bone artifacts. Meanwhile, aneurysm rupture risk is influenced by multiple factors including age, comorbidities, aneurysm morphology, and location. Traditional scoring systems or statistical models struggle to capture nonlinear relationships among these variables. There is an urgent need for an auxiliary method that can simultaneously perform image segmentation and clinical risk stratification.

To address this need, we divide the problem into two tasks: segmentation and prediction. For the segmentation task, we employ a 3D Attention U-Net as the base architecture, incorporating an ASPP multi-scale context module and 3D coordinate attention. Attention gates are introduced in the decoder to filter encoder features, enhancing the capture of small-target aneurysms. The loss function combines binary cross-entropy with Dice loss and its variants, balancing classification accuracy and region overlap. During inference, patch-based predictions are stitched back to the original space. For the rupture risk prediction task, we design a cross-attention-based deep tabular model for clinical information and imaging-derived features, and further integrate it with traditional machine learning models to construct a fusion model. Based on this, we integrate the segmentation and risk prediction pipelines through a configuration-driven approach into an interactive GUI program for algorithm demonstration, completing the entire process from volume data reading to auxiliary analysis.

Experimental results show that the segmentation model achieves a Dice coefficient of 0.8659 and IoU of 0.75 on the validation set. Under fixed training, validation, and test splits, the fusion model achieves the highest F1 score of 0.7330 and AUC of 0.8568; the proposed cross-attention deep tabular model achieves F1 score of 0.7266 and AUC of 0.8546, with overall performance close to the best results and superior to traditional methods such as logistic regression, support vector machines, and k-nearest neighbors. The results demonstrate that the proposed method can effectively assist in aneurysm segmentation and risk stratification, showing good practical application value.
